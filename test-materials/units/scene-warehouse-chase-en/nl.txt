用例：3D 预演 · 自然语言输入 · 英文模板（语言覆盖）
入口：scene-3d 节点 → 自然语言描述
预期：英文输入同样产出结构化 SceneScript；空间词（left/right/deep）正确落位

SCENE: A dim warehouse with tall storage racks forming narrow aisles. A single
overhead work light in the middle. Cold industrial lighting.

CHARACTERS: A courier in an orange vest starts near the light. A chaser in a
dark hoodie starts at the far end of the central aisle.

ACTION: The chaser runs down the aisle toward the courier. The courier dodges
behind a rack and peeks out.

CAMERA: Start with a low angle at the aisle end looking up at the racks. Cut
to a tracking shot following the chaser. Finish on a close-up of the courier
peeking.

STYLE: Tense, industrial thriller mood.
